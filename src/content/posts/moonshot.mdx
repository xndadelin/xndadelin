moonshot *was* a 4-day hackathon in florida visiting kennedy space center and universal studios! it was an amazing experience and i had a lot of fun!

---

## kennedy space center

we got to see the space shuttle atlantis, the apollo/saturn v center, the command center, the huge buildings where they build the rockets, and a lot more! seeing all this space history up close was incredible.

## universal studios

the only thing i cared about was the rollercoasters lmao! i only went on the hulk one because time was really tight, but it was so fun and really intense. it was my first rollercoaster ever and the first time i waited an hour in a line for anything — but it was worth it! i also got to see the harry potter world, which was really cool! i'm not a huge harry potter fan, but i knew enough of the lore to appreciate it all coming to life. unforgettable!

## my projects

how did i get there? well, i worked ~120h on multiple projects! check out [pydecodr](/pydecodr), nextctf, and other projects on my [github](https://github.com/xndadelin)!
